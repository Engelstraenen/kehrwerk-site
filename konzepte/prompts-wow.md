# KEHRWERK – Runde 3 „attraktiver“ (Eugen 23.11.2026: Mix A „scheiße langweilig“)
Gemeinsamer Kopf (HEAD) + Personen-Block (PEOPLE) + Ende (END) wie unten, je Variante eigener Teil.

HEAD: Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic screen state of the same spectacular award-winning website for a fictional modern German chimney sweep company called KEHRWERK (spelled exactly K-E-H-R-W-E-R-K). The design is bold and breathtaking like a top campaign website of Nike or Apple: oversized typography that overlaps and interacts with the photos (people and objects partly in front of giant letters), dramatic cinematic lighting, strong depth of field, motion blur, flying particles and light trails that show movement, every panel a different striking composition, never a plain standard layout, never boring.

PEOPLE: No clichés: the chimney sweeps wear modern black functional workwear like real tradespeople today (black softshell jacket, black work trousers, gloves, a red fall-protection harness, a small headlamp). NO top hat, NO brass buttons, NO traditional costume, NO lucky charms, NO clover, NO pig.

END: Short German headlines only, spelled exactly as given. No real brand logos, no prices, no euro signs, no phone numbers. Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels.
